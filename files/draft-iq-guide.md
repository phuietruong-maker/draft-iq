# Draft IQ — Quick-Start Guide

*Snake Draft Intelligence — your live command center for fantasy football drafts.*

Draft IQ helps you draft smarter by tracking who's left, flagging value, and warning you about runs, tier cliffs, and bye-week clashes — in real time. It works great on its own or alongside a Yahoo / ESPN / Sleeper draft.

---

## Setup (before the draft)

**1. Load your players**
On the setup screen, choose one:
- **⬇ Fetch from Sleeper** — pulls a free, current player pool (no login).
- **Paste** your own rankings into the box — CSV, tab-separated, or copied straight from a spreadsheet.
- **Upload CSV** — same format, from a file.
- **Sample** — loads a small demo list so you can explore.

Each row only needs a **name** and a **position** (QB/RB/WR/TE/K/DST — "DEF" also works). Team, rank, and bye week fill in automatically. Column order doesn't matter, and a header row is fine.

**2. Set your league**
- **Teams** — how many are in your league.
- **Your draft slot** — which pick position is yours (this drives all your personal alerts).
- **Roster spots** — adjust if your league differs from the defaults.

**3. Press START DRAFT.**

---

## During the draft

### The one habit that matters: log every pick
Draft IQ doesn't connect to Yahoo, so it stays accurate by *you* marking each player as they're taken. Keep it open on a second screen (phone/tablet) next to your real draft and mirror the picks.

**Fastest way — the ⚡ quick-pick bar:**
1. Type a few letters of the drafted player's name (`chase`, `mcca`, even `j chase`).
2. Press **Enter**.
3. They're off the board. The input refocuses for the next pick.

Use **↑ / ↓** to choose between matches, **Esc** to clear. You can also tap **Draft** on any player in the list.

The board follows **snake order** automatically and always knows who's on the clock.

## Reading the intelligence

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Green header** | It's your turn to pick. |
| **💎 value** | This player is still available well past their expected draft position — a potential steal. |
| **🏃 Positional run** | One position is flying off the board (3+ of the last 8 picks). Tells you if that position is thinning out. |
| **⛰ Tier cliff** | A position's top tier is almost empty and there's a big drop coming. Grab now or wait a full tier. |
| **⚠ Bye-week clash** | A player you just drafted shares a bye week with others on your roster — you'd be short that week. |
| **↗ Reach** | You drafted someone notably earlier than their rank; they might've lasted to your next turn. |

Your **roster** and **remaining position needs** stay visible on the right the whole time, with bye-week clashes flagged in red.

---

## Fixing mistakes
Tap **Undo** in the header to reverse the last pick. You can do this repeatedly to walk back several picks.

---

## Quick tips
- Set your **draft slot** correctly — your bye, reach, and best-available-for-you alerts all depend on it.
- Skim the colored strips between picks; they're designed to be read in the seconds between selections.
- The alert thresholds (what counts as a run, a value, a reach) are tuned for a typical league — if they feel too chatty or too quiet for yours, they can be adjusted.
